LONGPAN COAST — THIRD-PARTY NOTICES

All visible terrain, plants, animals and textures are generated locally by code. No stock photographs, stock models, Poly Haven textures or Rocketbox characters are used by the runtime. The main scene contains no person model.

TERRAIN DATA

Terrain Tiles by Mapzen/Tilezen, hosted by AWS Open Data.
https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/
Source and license guidance: https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md

Required upstream attribution for this area:
- SRTM data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey.
- Global ETOPO1 terrain data U.S. National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration.

The bundled Terrarium elevations are low-resolution geographic constraints, not engineering survey data. Source tile URLs and metadata are included in src/data/elevation.json.
